Privacy notice

Last updated: October 15, 2026

RIPS is a Solana pack product. Users may connect a wallet and pay real SOL to a project treasury to open packs. Collection items are recorded in your account after payment verification. NFT or SPL minting may be added in a later release.

Pack opens require an on-chain SOL transfer. Inventory entries are account records tied to verified payments. Unless an NFT/SPL mint is enabled, collectibles are not automatically transferable on-chain.

Information that may be stored

  • Without signing in: browser local storage may hold temporary UI state.
  • When you sign in: email/session via Supabase; display name; wallet public key; collection; activity; payment signatures.
  • Leaderboard: display name, item counts, and estimated collection value may be shown publicly. Emails are not published.
  • On-chain: SOL transfers and memos are public on Solana by design.
  • Technical data: hosting and auth providers process logs needed to run and secure the service.

How information is used

To authenticate you, verify pack payments, store your collection, operate leaderboards, and secure the product. It is not sold.
