Privacy notice

Last updated: October 15, 2026

RIPS LAB is a demo token-pack experience. It does not issue real tokens, hold funds, connect wallets, or submit blockchain transactions.

Information that may be stored

  • When you use the demo without signing in: your simulated balance, collected demo items, and activity history are stored in your browser's local storage.
  • When you sign in: Supabase Authentication processes your email address and session. RIPS LAB may store your display name, simulated balance, demo collection, and activity history in its Supabase database so your demo can follow your account across devices.
  • Basic technical data: the hosting and authentication providers may process technical information needed to deliver, protect, and troubleshoot the service, according to their own policies.

How information is used

Information is used to sign you in, show your profile, save your simulated collection, and operate and secure this demo. Demo items and balances have no real-world monetary value.

Storage and sharing

Anonymous demo data remains in the browser where it was created. Signed-in demo data is stored by the project's Supabase service. Access controls are intended to restrict account records to the relevant signed-in user. The hosting provider may process requests to serve the site. This notice does not promise that third-party providers never process technical data; review their current privacy terms for details.

Your choices

  • You can clear anonymous demo data by using the in-app reset control or clearing this site's browser storage.
  • You can sign out from the Account panel.
  • For questions or account-data requests, contact the project through the RIPS LAB GitHub issue page. Do not post private account information publicly; ask for a private contact route instead.

Children and real-money use

This is a demo, not a financial service. Do not submit payment details, wallet recovery phrases, private keys, or other secrets. No real-money features are offered.

Changes

This notice may be updated as the demo changes. The date above will be revised when material changes are made.

Important: RIPS LAB is a simulated experience. Demo balances, token names, and token values are illustrative only.
