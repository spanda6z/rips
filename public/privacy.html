Privacy notice

Last updated: October 15, 2026

RIPS LAB is a demo-only token-pack experience. It does not issue real tokens, hold funds, connect wallets, accept payments, or submit blockchain transactions.

Demo balances and collectibles are simulated. They have no market price and are not redeemable for money, tokens, or other value.

Information that may be stored

  • Without signing in: simulated balance, collected demo items, and activity history stay in your browser’s local storage.
  • When you sign in: Supabase Authentication processes your email and session. RIPS LAB may store your display name, simulated balance, demo collection, and activity so the demo can follow your account across devices.
  • Leaderboard (demo): a public aggregate of display name, item count, and simulated collection value may be shown. Email addresses are not published.
  • Technical data: hosting and auth providers may process logs and diagnostics needed to run and secure the service under their policies.

How information is used

Information is used only to sign you in, show your profile, save your simulated collection, operate the demo leaderboard, and keep the service secure. It is not sold.

Storage and sharing

Anonymous data stays in the browser. Signed-in data is stored in the project’s Supabase database with Row Level Security so each account only reaches its own collection and profile. The hosting provider (for example Vercel) processes requests to serve the site.

Retention

Local storage remains until you clear site data or reset the demo. Signed-in records remain until you request deletion or the project is shut down.

Your choices

  • Use the demo without creating an account.
  • Sign out at any time.
  • Reset local demo data from the app.
  • Request deletion of a signed-in account’s stored demo data via the contact address below.

Contact
